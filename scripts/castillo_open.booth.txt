Articulation 190.4 WPM + 4.10 s of inter-sentence pause = 177 WPM speaking rate.

MARKS
  [WORD]   hero - the one operative word in the sentence. Everything else serves it.
  (word)   throwaway - de-emphasised, faster, quieter. Undercut it.
  /        breath (4 total, 0.32-0.46 s each)
  v        FULL cadence - land the period
  \        HALF cadence - step down, keep moving
  ^        SUSPENSION - hold it up, do NOT resolve, then pause
  ~        THROWAWAY ending - drop level and speed through the tail
  .30      pause after, in seconds. Nothing exceeds 0.52.

STANDING NOTES

Don't sing it. No two adjacent sentences share an ending shape, and
across 13 sentences the four shapes run 4 FULL / 3 HALF / 4 SUSPENSION
/ 2 THROWAWAY. Terminal-move distribution: median -0.3 st, q1 -1.4,
q3 +0.9 (reference human narrator: -0.31 / -1.31 / +1.08).

Land the period only three times. S3, S8, S13. Everywhere else the
sentence hands off rather than closes.

Never stress a token because it is capitalised or numeric. Dates,
years, ages, dollar figures, street names, county names, court numbers
and the judge's name are all throwaway in this script. That habit is
the single loudest tell.

Say names as one unit. "Erik Michael Moody" is one word with one mild
stress. "The Bexar County Sheriff's Office" is one word. "A one hundred
thousand dollar corporate surety bond" is one word.

Pauses stay short. Max 0.52 s. Authority is in the contour, not the gap.
